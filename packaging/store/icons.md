# Frosty icons

The icon theme `frosty` is part of Frosty, a frosted glass theme for KDE Plasma 6: https://github.com/Lukk17/frosty

It is made from these icon themes. Which icon comes from where, and every change with its date, is listed in `vendor/icons/CHANGES.md` of the Frosty project.

1. Infinity-Dark-Icons by L4ki, from https://github.com/L4ki/Infinity-Plasma-Themes at commit `e40490e79decb2f76d8e30c737bf7065f3112715`, GPL-3.0-only. Infinity itself contains Breeze icons by Uri Herrera and the other Breeze authors (https://invent.kde.org/frameworks/breeze-icons, LGPL-3.0-or-later) and some GNOME symbolic icons by the GNOME Project (https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/adwaita-icon-theme, LGPL-3.0-only or CC BY-SA 3.0 United States).
2. candy-icons by EliverLara, from https://github.com/EliverLara/candy-icons at commit `8d21022f3b77d6a3b44f9a4a1ed26d6a974c41f6`, GPL-3.0-only.
3. Sours-Full-Color by tully-t, from https://github.com/tully-t/Sours at commit `a7027ca599d1cba293d769fb23f3c225dd1edbc0`, GPL-3.0-only. Sours descends from BeautyLine by sajjad606 (https://store.kde.org/p/1425426, GPL version 3) and from candy-icons.
