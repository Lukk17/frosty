# Frosty color scheme

The color scheme `Frosty` is part of Frosty, a frosted glass theme for KDE Plasma 6: https://github.com/Lukk17/frosty

It is a changed copy of `color-schemes/Layan.colors` by vinceliuice, from https://github.com/vinceliuice/Layan-kde at commit `a0b6a4956022276aee33309b2e07d1d0ef3db30c`. Every change and its date is listed in `vendor/color-scheme/CHANGES.md` of the Frosty project.
